Newsletter

You can sign up for emails about new games with the form on this site or on Buttondown’s page. The form sends your email address straight to Buttondown (USA), which stores it to send the emails. You confirm the signup by email, every email has a link to unsubscribe, and I use the address for nothing else. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Buttondown may process your address in the USA. See Buttondown’s privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you is stored, and have it corrected, deleted or restricted. You can object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the one for Hesse, HBDI.
